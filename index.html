About Raj Shamani

Raj Shamani is one of India’s leading young entrepreneurs, content creators, and motivational speakers. He started his journey at the age of 16 by building a small trading business, which later scaled into a successful brand. Today, he is known for simplifying money, business, and personal growth for millions of people through his content, books, and speeches.

Raj has delivered 200+ speeches in 26+ countries and has shared stages with global icons. His podcast "Figuring Out" is among India’s top business podcasts, where he interviews some of the smartest founders, creators, and leaders.

Career Journey

Early Life & Start

Raj started selling products locally while studying in school. Coming from a modest background, he learned business by observing his father and experimenting with small opportunities.

Building a Business

He built a distribution business and expanded it into multiple regions. His practical experience in sales and growth helped him understand how Indian businesses actually work.

Rise as a Speaker

Raj became one of the youngest Indians to speak on global stages, including the United Nations. His speeches inspire people to build skills, take action, and think independently.

Creator & Influencer

Raj gained massive popularity by sharing insights on money, business, self-growth, and entrepreneurship. Today he reaches millions of people across Instagram, YouTube, and podcasts.
